## 3. PRO-TIPS FOR VIDEO RECORDING SUCCESS

### Lighting, Audio & Framing:
* **Camera Angle:** Eye-level (put your laptop on books or use a tripod for your smartphone). Never shoot from below your chin.
* **Lighting:** Sit facing a window or use a ring light placed directly behind the camera. Do NOT have bright lights behind you.
* **Audio Quality:** Use wired earphones (Apple EarPods) or a lapel/mic plugged into your phone. Crisp audio accounts for 60% of perceived competence.
* **Dress Code:** Wear a dark navy, charcoal, or pink-accented collared shirt / polo / blazer. Look like a performance analyst at an IPL auction desk.

### Body Language & Voice Tone:
* **The "Giles Lindsay Composure":** Do not rush. Speak at 140–150 words per minute. Pause for 0.5 seconds between major stats (e.g., *"10.29 runs an over... [pause]... with an 8.4-over wicketless drift"*).
* **No Nervous Up-Talk:** End statements with a downward inflection (declarative authority), not like you are asking a question.
* **Eye Contact:** Look straight into the camera lens, not at your own face on the screen.

### Screen Recording Technique:
* Use **OBS Studio** or **Loom** to record with a Picture-in-Picture bubble (your face in bottom-right corner, your live Streamlit dashboard full screen).
* While speaking, click the tabs in sync with your script:
  * Mention Overs 7–11 -> Show Tab 1 KPI cards.
  * Mention Bayesian shrinkage -> Show Tab 2 Leaderboard.
  * Mention Matt Henry & Suyash -> Show Tab 3 Plan B and Tab 8 Auction Sliders.
  * Mention Stadium physics -> Show Tab 5 9-Venue Microclimate Matrix.
  * Mention Monte Carlo -> Show Tab 7 Bar chart.
