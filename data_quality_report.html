2. Cricket Accounting & Special Event Protocols

CRITICAL AUDIT FINDING: In ball-by-ball cricket datasets, improper credit for extras and wickets corrupts bowler and batter pressure indices. RR-EDGE enforces strict ICC-standard accounting protocols.
Event / Rule Forensic Finding in Data Pack RR-EDGE Accounting Protocol
Wides (7,714 balls) extras_type = 'wide', runs = 0, extras1_runs >= 1 Not counted as legal delivery; not credited as ball faced by batter; full runs charged to bowler.
No-Balls / Overstep (576 balls) extras_type = 'Overstep', extras1_runs = 1 Not a legal ball for bowler; counted as ball faced for batter if stroke made; bowler charged 1 + bat runs.
Byes & Leg-byes (3,277 balls) extras_type in ('byes', 'legbyes') Counted as legal delivery; added to batting team total; EXCLUDED from bowler's runs conceded.
Bowler Wickets vs Field Wickets Bowled (1,775), Caught (7,557), LBW (709), Stump (214), Hit Wicket (17) Credited to bowler wicket count. Run-outs (600) and Retire Outs (6) strictly excluded from bowler credit.
Super Overs (38 balls) innings = 3 (25 balls) and innings = 4 (13 balls) Flagged as Maximum Pressure (Pressure Index = 100); analyzed in high-leverage evaluation suite.
Rain-Interrupted & Abandoned Matches 16 matches abandoned without play; 25 matches with revised_overs / revised_target Target dynamically re-indexed against revised_target and revised_overs for chase pressure calculation.
